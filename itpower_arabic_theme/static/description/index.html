Arabic Backend Theme

الثيم العربي لنظام Odoo

A clean backend theme for Odoo Community, designed right-to-left first. It gives your users an apps sidebar, a full-screen apps menu with a search that understands Arabic, your own colors and the IBM Plex Sans Arabic font. It works just as well in English.

What you get

المميزات

Right-to-left first

Built and tested in Arabic. The sidebar sits on the right, arrows and highlights follow the reading direction, and lines are spaced for Arabic script. Switch a user to English and everything mirrors back.

Apps sidebar

Every app is one click away from any screen. Hover an icon to see its name, or expand the bar to show all names. Each user's choice is remembered in their browser.

Full-screen apps menu

A greeting with the user's name and today's date, your company background and a large grid of apps. Open it with the apps button or Alt+H.

Search that understands Arabic

Search every app and menu by name. Letters people type interchangeably are treated as the same (أ إ آ ا, ة ه, ى ي) and diacritics are ignored. Use the arrow keys and Enter to open a result.

Your colors

Pick the top bar, sidebar, buttons and highlight colors per company in Settings. One click brings the default theme back.

IBM Plex Sans Arabic

A clear font for Arabic and English text, numbers and tables, shipped inside the module. No external font service is called.

Background and tab icon

Upload your own apps menu background and browser tab icon (favicon) per company.

Phones and tablets

On small screens the sidebar steps aside and the apps menu takes over, with a grid sized for touch.

Arabic and English included

The theme's own labels come translated into Arabic.

Installation

  1. Copy the itpower_arabic_theme folder into your addons path and restart Odoo.
  2. Go to Apps, update the apps list and install IT Power Arabic Backend Theme.
  3. Optional: set your colors, background and tab icon in Settings › General Settings › Backend Theme.

Right-to-left pages need the rtlcss tool on the Odoo server, as for any Odoo installation used in Arabic. The theme replaces the standard apps dropdown, so do not install it together with another backend theme.
